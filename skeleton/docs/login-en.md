# Sign in

## Purpose

Demonstrate entry to an authenticated area with email and password fields. Use fictional credentials only.

## Expected behaviour

Both fields are required; the email must be valid and the password needs at least eight characters. Submission opens /dashboard with an in-memory example session. Links reach signup and password recovery.

## Prototype boundary

All data and sessions are local examples. No backend, email delivery or persistent account is implied. Adapt this specification to your own requirements.
